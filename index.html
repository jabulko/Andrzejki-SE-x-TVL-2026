Andrzejki Wax Pouring

In Poland, Andrzejki is a night of fortune-telling and a little bit of magic.

One traditional ritual involves pouring melted wax through the opening of a key into cold water. The wax hardens into a mysterious shape, and its shadow is said to reveal little secrets about what the future might hold.
